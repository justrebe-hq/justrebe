Quick Answers · Common Objections

"How is this different from other PD?"
ReBe Ed is whole-health — not just literacy or SEL. The EPICS framework (Emotional, Physical, Intellectual, Cultural, Social/Spiritual) covers every pathway a student and educator needs. Aligned to the Science of Reading.
"What does 'EPICS' stand for?"
Emotional · Physical · Intellectual · Cultural · Social/Spiritual. The five pathways of whole-health wellness that every ReBe Ed offering is built on.
"Do you have funding sources?"
Title IV-A, ESSER III, mental-health grants, Title I (ask about qualifying rate). We accept purchase orders with Net 30 terms.
"How long does onboarding take?"
A member of our team will follow up soon to walk them through next steps. Don't quote a specific timeline — we haven't finalized it yet.
"Can we start with just one part?"
Yes. Start with Thrive if leading with educator wellness, K-2 Health Boosts if student-first. Transform is the whole system — recommend to schools ready for both.
"What's the 3-5 pilot?"
Every buyer of K-2 Health Boosts gets free access to the 3-5 pilot. Their feedback shapes the final 3-5 product.
