Educator Wellness · Thrive

The Lights + Camera package.

Give your educators the tools to be well themselves — so they can show up whole for their students.

A three-stage experience that opens the conversation without threatening it. Educators start with awareness, deepen at their own pace, and come into community only when they're ready.

Lights is the entry. Camera is the self-directed library. Action is the live community — and it's an optional add-on when your team is ready.

1 Entry point

Lights — Awareness

A 3-hour PD experience — in person or online — that opens the conversation without threatening it.

  • In-person Inservice · 3 hours · whole faculty · mixed educators + admin (*travel added if in person)
  • Or Online delivery · same 3-hour experience, live facilitated
  • ACEs overview · EPICS pathways · genre portraits · Health Boost demo · Science of Reading foundations · Literacy LABS preview
  • No sharing required — every activity is private and personal
2 Self-directed

Camera — Clearinghouse

A private, on-demand digital library where educators go deeper on their own terms — cameras off, no live participation required.

  • EPICS video library · 25 concepts · 3-minute videos · always accessible
  • Resource links for every concept — curated readings, tools, next steps
  • Webinar on-ramps — optional live participation, easy entry
  • Five self-discovery journeys: Receive · Reveal · Reflect · Release · Relaunch
3 Community + 1:1

Action — The rewrite

Where the story actually changes. Educators move from private self-discovery into live human community — supported, seen, and no longer carrying it alone.

  • ReBe ReFresh Live groups · 5-week small-group experience · online · live
  • EPICS topical community groups · ongoing groups by health domain (Emotional, Physical, Intellectual, Cultural, Social/Spiritual)
  • Up to 20 private 1:1 Confidant sessions — administrator option, personal support for educators who need it
  • Optional add-on. Add to Thrive or Transform.
