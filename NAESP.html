The reality

Unseen stress becomes visible everywhere.

When educators and students carry unseen stress, it shows up in burnout, behavior, and disengagement. Collectively, these challenges constitute a drastic crisis in which students lack adequate literacy, life skills, and wellness support to thrive, while educators are also contending with unaddressed adverse childhood experiences (ACEs), burnout, and increasing attrition rates.

What is EPICS?

EPICS is the re·be framework for whole-health wellness — five pathways every educator and student can grow in: Emotional Physical Intellectual Cultural Social / Spiritual

What is ReBe Ed?

For your students.

An ACE-aware wellness platform built for your students.

Built around EPICS — Emotional, Physical, Intellectual, Cultural, and Social health — ReBe Ed revitalizes the wellbeing, social connection, and productivity of educators, students, and the broader school community.

Through 7–10 minute Health Boosts and Science-of-Reading-based Literacy LABS™ (guided by BB the Bee), schools get practical tools for healthier, more connected communities.

The result: students who can name what they feel, classrooms that move from reactive to responsive, and classrooms that are transforming lives.

What is ReBe Ed?

For your educators.

An ACE-aware wellness platform built for your educators.

Before your educators can hold space for the children in your building, someone needs to hold space for them. That's why Act II of the ReBe Ed experience is a story of them — an ACE-informed, root-cause self-discovery journey grounded in decades of therapeutic practice.

It moves through three phases — Lights (awareness), Camera (engagement), Action (activation). Educators name what's really going on, get language and tools that fit their day, and turn awareness into practice that sticks in the classroom and beyond.

Your educators get to rewrite their own story — because how they show up for themselves is how they show up for their students.
