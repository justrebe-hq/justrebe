Cancel any time. Two clicks. No fee. No guilt. Your access continues through the end of your current billing period, and then it just stops. Come back whenever.
What if I miss a live session?
Every session is recorded and posted to your library within 24 hours. Watch on your schedule. Rewatch the ones that landed. You don't lose anything by missing a live.
When are the in-person pop-up events?
A few times a year, in different cities. Announced first to members with priority ticket access. First one is being planned for late 2026 — details coming.
Can I try it for one month?
Yes. The monthly plan is exactly that — try it for $30, decide by the end of the month. If it's not for you, cancel. If it is, we'll be glad you stayed.
Can members book private 1:1 sessions?
Yes — private 1:1s with any confidant are $197/session and available separately. That's on a different page.
